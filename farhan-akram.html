Biography

Dr. Farhan Akram is an assistant professor in the Department of Pathology and Clinical Bioinformatics at Erasmus MC in Rotterdam, where he leads the Computational Pathology Group. He develops AI models that analyse whole-slide images to classify tumours, estimate recurrence risk and predict treatment response. His recent studies have examined whether lung tumour biopsies can help predict recurrence after surgery and whether routine histology can identify molecular subtypes of uveal melanoma.

As principal investigator of AI3PATH, Dr. Akram studies how three-dimensional tissue architecture can inform tumour assessment. He also investigates whether combining pathology images with radiology, spatial transcriptomics and clinical data improves predictions, and contributes to a project using AI to predict kidney transplant rejection.

Dr. Akram earned his PhD cum laude from Universitat Rovira i Virgili in 2017, where he developed active contour methods for image segmentation. He held postdoctoral appointments at A*STAR in Singapore and Khalifa University in Abu Dhabi, and developed computer vision methods for microscopy at mil-kin Inc. in Tokyo. He holds a University Teaching Qualification from Erasmus University Rotterdam and supervises graduate researchers in computational pathology.
